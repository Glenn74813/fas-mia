## Public sources checked (27 October 2026)

Public sources were searched for the open items below, to settle what can be settled without MIAA and to narrow
the questions that remain. Nothing in the system was changed by these findings. Some sources are secondary
(law firms, consultancies, vendors) and are marked so; confirm them against the official text before relying on
them.

| Item | What the public record shows | What MIAA still has to supply |
|---|---|---|
| C-07 framework, chart and statement formats | MIAA's 2023 COA Annual Audit Report states that its financial statements are prepared "in accordance with Philippine Financial Reporting Standards (PFRS)", so MIAA reports as a government business enterprise under COA Circular 2015-003 (amended by 2022-003), not under PPSAS. Its statements are the Statements of Financial Position, Comprehensive Income, Changes in Equity and Cash Flows, with notes. Their headings follow COA's government chart of accounts: Business and Service Income; Shares, Donations and Grants; Gains; Personnel Services; Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses; Financial Expenses; Non-Cash Expenses; Income Tax Expense; Subsidy to Other Funds; Government Equity; Retained Earnings; Inter-Agency Payables; Trust Liabilities. The chart for government corporations is the Revised Chart of Accounts for Government Corporations (2019), issued by COA Circular 2020-002 and updated by Circular 2021-005 (Annex A, provided by MIAA's side as `docs/chart.pdf`); it is now loaded in development (C-22). | MIAA's own sub-accounts under COA's codes, and the statement templates COA expects (lapsing schedules, notes). Development's statement templates (`RCA-SFP`, `RCA-SCI`, `RCA-SCF`, `RCA-SCE`) use these headings; their groupings are proposals. |
| C-10 budget definitions and allocation keys | GOCC corporate operating budgets (COB) follow DBM Corporate Budget Circular No. 24 (4 November 2022) under EO 518 (1979): prepared before the fiscal year, approved by the governing board, submitted to DBM by 31 March with DBM's forms. | The meaning of reservation, commitment and obligation in MIAA's practice, its COB line structure, and the overhead allocation keys (internal; not published). |
| C-05, C-13 retention and lawful purge | Disposal of public records is governed by RA 9470 (2007) and the National Archives' General Records Disposition Schedule for records common to all agencies (NAP General Circulars 1 and 2, 20 January 2009); a newer NAP circular (General Circular No. 5, 2023) is listed on the NAP site, and whether it replaces the 2009 schedule was not confirmed.
